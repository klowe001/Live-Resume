## Regulatory changes

### Tariffs: BIS tightened the "U.S. content" test for steel, aluminum and copper
BIS issued guidance to CBP defining U.S. content for USMCA-qualifying goods of Canada and Mexico under Proclamation 11032 (the June 1, 2026 adjustment to the aluminum, steel and copper tariff regimes). U.S. content means parts wholly obtained, entirely produced or substantially transformed in the United States, plus, to the extent not already counted, value attributable to steel melted and poured or aluminum and copper smelted and cast in the U.S. **Effective 12:01 a.m. ET Oct 14, 2026**, applying to goods entered or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption on or after that time. Hits anyone sourcing structural metal, enclosures, castings or wiring through Canadian or Mexican tier ones. ([Federal Register, Oct 14](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/10/14/2026-20938/guidance-to-us-customs-and-border-protection-regarding-the-assessment-of-us-content-for-purposes-of))

### FCC Upper C-band rule takes effect, satellite transition plans due Nov 5
The Upper C-band order (3.98–4.2 GHz) published in the Federal Register on Oct 19, with the amendatory instructions to 47 CFR 27.1412(e) and 27.1419 effective that day. Satellite operators must file transition plans for clearing 4.0–4.16 GHz by **Nov 5, 2026**. Affects FSS operators with Upper C-band downlinks and the terrestrial licensees inheriting the spectrum. ([Federal Register, Oct 19](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/10/19/2026-21324/upper-c-band-398-42-ghz-expanding-flexible-use-of-the-37-to-42-ghz-band))

### NRC renewed the AP1000 design certification through 2066 and made Vogtle 4 the reference plant
Issued as a direct final rule with a companion proposed rule on Oct 14, extending certification from 2046 to 2066 and folding Vogtle 3 and 4 construction lessons into the certified design so future applicants need not re-license changes already evaluated. **Effective Dec 28, 2026** absent significant adverse comment; comments close Nov 13. Matters to anyone filing a new AP1000 COL, and to the Polish and Dutch programs now ordering long-lead items. ([Federal Register, Oct 14](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/10/14/2026-21030/ap1000-design-certification-renewal), [ANS, Oct 16](https://www.ans.org/news/2026-10-16/article-8493/nrc-oks-westinghouse-ap1000-design-certification-revision-renewal/))

### New drone exclusion zone along the U.S.–Mexico border
DOT, the Department of War and DHS jointly barred unauthorized drone flights from the surface to 400 ft AGL within one nautical mile north of the Mexico border, implemented through FAA NOTAMs, with CBP approval requested at least 48 hours ahead and carve-outs for law enforcement, firefighting and search and rescue. The effective date is inconsistent across sources: the departments' announcement points to Oct 16, while NOTAM data reportedly shows 0000 UTC Oct 20 running to Oct 19, 2027. Anyone flying survey, ag, pipeline or infrastructure work in the border corridor should treat it as live now. ([The Rio Times, Oct 17](https://www.riotimesonline.com/us-mexico-border-drone-ban-cartels-2026/))

### FAA Part 108 BVLOS is still sitting at OIRA
No publication, no effective date. Reporting as of this month puts it at roughly 90 days under OMB review and months past target, and no OIRA action date falls in the next ten days. The single largest pending variable for every BVLOS business model remains unresolved. ([Status tracker, Oct 2026](https://www.droneranker.com/faa-part-108-bvlos-rule-status/))

### FCC added Pentagon letters to the DJI and Autel dockets without changing their status
On Oct 14 the Public Safety and Homeland Security Bureau placed previously submitted Department of War Section 1709 letters into ET Dockets 26-22 (DJI) and 26-23 (Autel), publishing only redacted unclassified cover pages. The FCC states it did not rely on those letters when adding the firms to the Covered List, so there is no new restriction; DJI's reconsideration petition and Autel's application for review are both still pending. ([DroneDJ, Oct 15](https://dronedj.com/2026/10/15/fcc-dji-ban-pentagon-letters/))

### ITAR: USML Category XX(a) modification took effect Oct 19
The DDTC final rule narrowing Category XX(a) (submersible and oceanographic equipment) became effective **Oct 19, 2026**. Relevant if you build or export subsea or maritime autonomy hardware. ([Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/09/18/2026-19211/international-traffic-in-arms-regulations-modification-of-us-munitions-list-category-xxa))

### Blue Origin asked the FAA to raise LC-36A from 12 to 50 launches per year
Thirty-day comment period open, virtual public hearing **Oct 22**, comments close **Oct 30**. Part of a Space Coast buildout including a second pad for the 9x4 New Glenn variant and a $330M, 1.37M sq ft Rocket Park expansion due summer 2027. ([Spaceflight Now, Oct 16](https://spaceflightnow.com/2026/10/16/blue-origin-to-add-new-florida-launch-pads-seeks-aggressive-increase-in-new-glenn-flight-rate/))

### Robot safety: South Korea cleared the first multi-humanoid factory trial
Korea's industrial-convergence regulatory sandbox approved a 20-humanoid deployment for Robros, exempting it from safety rules written around fencing workers off from fixed industrial robots. Sites span semiconductor packaging cleaning (Nepes), auto body part positioning (Sungwoo Hitech), shipbuilding material stacking (HD Hyundai Samho) and cold-chain packaging. This is the first real regulatory precedent for unfenced humanoids on a production floor, and OSHA has produced nothing comparable. ([Seoul Economic Daily, Oct 13](https://en.sedaily.com/finance/2026/10/13/korea-clears-first-factory-test-of-20-humanoid-robots))

### CHIPS: GF award amended, Commerce takes an equity stake
Up to $955M of GlobalFoundries' existing CHIPS award was reallocated against a raised $5B capital commitment at Malta NY and Burlington VT, covering silicon photonics, SiGe and what is described as the first U.S. source of silicon interposers for advanced packaging. The total award does not increase, and Commerce takes roughly a 4% equity stake in GF. ([Semiconductor Today, Oct 16](https://www.semiconductor-today.com/news_items/2026/oct/globalfoundries-161026.shtml))

### Germany launched a milestone-based fusion program
SPRIND will run a competitive, milestone-gated program for BMFTR: **€500M through 2029**, with public funding potentially rising to €2.5B thereafter, awards aimed mainly at start-ups and assessed by independent external reviewers, with a Technical Design Report for a demonstration plant as the final milestone. No participating companies named yet. ([ANS, Oct 14](https://www.ans.org/news/2026-10-14/article-8485/germany-launches-its-own-fusion-milestone-program/))

### NASA and DOE signed a 10-year space nuclear MOU
"Accelerating American Leadership in Space Nuclear Power and Propulsion" takes effect **Nov 1, 2026**, superseding a 2016 radioisotope MOU and a Dec 2025 space fission MOU, and covering fuel production, testing, launch integration and operations. Referenced milestones: the SR-1 Freedom nuclear-electric-propulsion launch in Dec 2028 and a lunar surface reactor launch by 2030. No funding figures. ([ANS, Oct 13](https://www.ans.org/news/2026-10-13/article-8482/nasa-and-doe-sign-space-nuclear-mou/))

### Quiet this week
No BIS entity-list or export-control rule, no DOE rule, and no NHTSA or OSHA rule among the rules and proposed rules published Oct 12–19. The only in-window BIS action was the U.S.-content notice above.
